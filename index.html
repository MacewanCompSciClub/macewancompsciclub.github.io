WHAT’S INVOLVED?

Join us for a 42 hour hackathon starting at 5:30pm Friday October 16th. Participants will be given a challenge to solve, build solutions and present their work to judges. There will be food too!

WHO CAN JOIN?

The event is free and open to all students of all skill levels. We will have different challenges (and prizes) based on experience.
